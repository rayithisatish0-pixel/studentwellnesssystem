📚 Student Self-Care Library

Curated by Clinical Staff

The 50/10 Focus & Sensory Rest Protocol

Study uninterrupted for 50 minutes with all phone notifications silenced. Follow with 10 minutes of complete sensory rest: walk, drink water, or stretch without looking at any screen. This preserves prefrontal cortex energy and prevents cognitive burnout.

Cognitive Defusion for Catastrophic Exam Thoughts

When an anxious thought arises like "I'm going to fail and ruin my future," silently alter the phrasing: "I notice that I am having the thought that I might fail." Separating your intrinsic self from transient cognitions instantly lowers acute anxiety.

Circadian Sleep Hygiene for Memory Consolidation

Long-term memory synthesis occurs during deep slow-wave and REM sleep cycles. Maintain a consistent bedtime window within 30 minutes every day and avoid bright screen exposure 45 minutes before sleep to trigger natural melatonin production.
