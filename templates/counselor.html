Institutional Early Warning Methodology

The Serene Minds early detection algorithm continuously scans longitudinal inputs:

  • PHQ-9 Score ≥ 15: Flags moderately severe to severe depressive symptomatology.
  • GAD-7 Score ≥ 15: Flags severe acute anxiety requiring grounding intervention.
  • ABSI Score ≥ 25: Flags critical academic burnout and emotional exhaustion.
  • Persistent Mood Degradation: Triggers if student logs ≤ 2.0 for 3+ consecutive entries or maintains a rolling 5-entry average below 2.2.
