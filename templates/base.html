🚨 Confidential Support & Emergency Helplines

If you or someone you know is in crisis or experiencing unbearable distress, please reach out to any of these free, 24/7 confidential resources:

National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or Text 988 (Available 24 hours every day, English & Spanish)

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a volunteer crisis counselor

Campus Wellness Center Urgent Walk-In

Pavilion Building 4, Suite 102 | Direct Line: (555) 019-4822

The Trevor Project (LGBTQ+ Crisis Line)

Call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678
