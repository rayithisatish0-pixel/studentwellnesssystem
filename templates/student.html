🧠 The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Method

When acute panic or exam anxiety strikes, anchor yourself in the present moment:

  • 5 things you can see around you
  • 4 things you can physically feel or touch
  • 3 things you can clearly hear
  • 2 things you can smell
  • 1 thing you can taste or positive self-affirmation

💤 Sleep Architecture for Students

Maintain cognitive retention during midterms and project crunches:

  • Keep a consistent sleep schedule within a 45-minute window
  • No caffeine within 7 hours of desired bedtime
  • Dim blue screen lights 60 minutes before resting
  • Expose your eyes to morning natural sunlight within 30 min of waking
